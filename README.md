## 📦 What Does This Tool Do?

Every photo you take contains hidden metadata. This includes:

- **GPS coordinates** – where the photo was taken
- **Camera model** – what device captured the image
- **Date and time** – when the photo was shot
- **Software information** – what app processed the image

When you share photos on social media, email, or cloud storage, this metadata travels with the image. Anyone who downloads your photo can see this information. This tool removes all of it in one simple step.
